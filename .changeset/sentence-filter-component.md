Add `SentenceFilter` (`@venture-ui/ui/SentenceFilter`): a filter that reads as a sentence, where each changeable word opens a list of options with optional counts, and fixed phrases can be removed as tokens. It works as a page heading or a small line of text, keeps 44 px touch targets without loosening the line spacing, and holds no filter state. Ships a showcase template for the Astryx CLI.
